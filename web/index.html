QuoteProof: is the quoted sentence really in the opinion?

Pick a US Supreme Court opinion, paste a sentence someone quoted from it, press Check. Nothing is installed and nothing is sent anywhere: the check runs in your browser on the opinion texts bundled with this page (21 opinions, two public sources each: Harvard CAP and Cornell LII). It runs the same verifyFromTexts code as the command line. The match rule is string match only: no legal reasoning, no judgement of whether the quote supports an argument.
